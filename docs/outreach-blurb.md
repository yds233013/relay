## B. Short email

**Subject:** Relay — a prototype for ERP migration QA

Hi [Name],

I've been exploring how the data-migration side of ERP implementations could scale, and built an
independent prototype called Relay to test the idea.

It takes legacy accounting data through migration QA to a verified go-live: deterministic
validation and reconciliation against the customer's own control reports, a work queue that turns
dozens of findings into the handful of decisions a person has to make, and a bounded investigator
that traces each issue to evidence and drafts a fix. Every material change goes through approval
with segregation of duties, and Relay re-runs its checks to prove the fix held before anyone signs
off.

It runs on fictional data, and the demo's investigator is a scripted replay rather than a live
model, but the controls, reconciliations and audit trail are real.

Live demo (no sign-in): https://web-production-9032d5.up.railway.app
Code: https://github.com/yds233013/relay

I'd love to hear how closely this matches the problems your implementation team actually runs
into.
